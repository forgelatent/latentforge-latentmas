## 3. Adopted wording for v0.4 (edit list; nothing applied)

All four readers found the same loophole in E2: the PASS sentence says "AND Gate 1, AND Gate 2" while the gates paragraph leaves sensitivity survival "open", so a run could be labelled PASS with a gate met under primary resampling only. Three readers supplied replacement text; ChatGPT's and Gemini 3.1 Pro's are near-identical. Merged:

- §7 Gates, replace "Whether a gate must also survive sensitivity to count as met is open; the primary endpoint must." with: **"A gate counts as met only if its 95% interval lies entirely above zero under both primary-group resampling and sensitivity-block resampling."** The gates heading's "both 95% group-bootstrap intervals" becomes "each evaluated with 95% intervals under both primary-group and sensitivity-block resampling" (ChatGPT).
- §8 Pass, replace with: **"Pass: primary-endpoint interval entirely above the pre-registered margin m under both primary-group and sensitivity-block resampling, AND Gate 1 and Gate 2 each met as defined in §7, AND L beats BR."** The parenthetical "proposed reading of v3's 'survives'" is dropped: the reading is now decided.
- §8 Fail, the labelled case, in ChatGPT's wording: **"The primary-group interval establishes an improvement exceeding the pre-registered margin. The sensitivity-block interval establishes a positive improvement but does not establish that the improvement exceeds that margin. Result: FAIL — the required advantage was not established under the registered sensitivity analysis."** The report shows the common point estimate and both intervals. What this licenses: positive superiority under both resampling schemes, and margin-exceeding superiority under primary groups; not E1's sentence. This replaces the shorter label proposed in the round 5 synthesis ("positive under sensitivity; margin not established").
- Design v3 Step 5's sentence "A PASS that does not survive is reported as not established" is read as covering the gates as well as the primary endpoint; no change to v3 is needed for this point, but the v3 amendment already opened for bridge nodes (decision 2) should say so in one line.
